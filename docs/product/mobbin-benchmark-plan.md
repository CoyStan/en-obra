# Mobbin-Benchmark Professionalization Plan

## Intent

Cuentas Claras should benchmark against polished mobile finance products, not
generic dashboards. Mobbin's public finance category is the product reference:
balance-first screens, compact tab bars, shallow surfaces, strong list rhythm,
clear money objects, and obvious next actions.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the active money question.
   The first group workspace should answer: who am I, which group is active,
   how much is open, who pays whom, and what can I do next.

2. Replace heavy stacked cards with a canvas and islands.
   Keep content, but reduce borders, card nesting, and visual noise. Use
   hairlines, soft washes, and list dividers where a full card is not needed.

3. Make money movement visible.
   Settlement routes, avatars, amount chips, and activity icons should carry
   meaning before the user reads every line.

4. Use Android-native structure.
   Bottom navigation, sheets, rows, chips, and 44px touch targets stay familiar.
   Premium polish comes from hierarchy and spacing, not decorative hero art.

5. Keep LATAM trust cues quiet but present.
   MXN, local-only/demo state, and "Sin pagos reales" must remain visible
   without dominating the screen.

6. Write for low cognitive load.
   Short labels, direct verbs, fewer paragraphs, and scannable metadata. The app
   should work for a tired user splitting dinner, rent, or a trip.

## Acceptance Bar

The app should look more like a real finance product: calm, structured,
confident, and legible. It should not look like a prototype, dense admin panel,
or landing page.
